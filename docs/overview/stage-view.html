What the screenshots show (0.41.0, 4K window)

  1. Most of the window is unused. The Overview is a column about 940 px wide: on the 4K window the right half is only the backdrop.
  2. The scene is a widget. In the first shot it is a 200 px card with five cards stacked under it (Branch and merge, two sessions, Where it stands, Build); the one thing to do, Decisions Needed, is 600 px below it, below the fold.
  3. It contradicts itself. The hero says Review · A session is working on it while both session cards say Idle: waiting for you (journeyModel counts an idle session as running).
  4. The Review step's chips float between two cards, belonging to neither.
  5. The same thing is said three or four times: the title in the rail, the head and each session card; the state as a chip, in the hero and in Where it stands; each document in the rail repeats the whole ticket title.
  6. Star Chart is cut by its card (rounded corners and edges), so it reads as a picture in a frame; in decide mode the ship orbits over the BUILD label.
  7. The head's buttons wrap to a second row under a truncated title.

The proposal: the Overview is a stage

  • One scene, the whole window. The theme's sky and ground are the page; the journey runs across the full width, centred in the free height, and the page never scrolls.
  • Top line: id, title, chips, and only Stages, Refresh and ⋯ (Cancel workflow, Archive). Under it the hero, large, in the theme's type: where it is, what is next.
  • The gate is the one place to act: the next step in the theme's words, what else can be done, a warning when there is one (uncommitted files), the Review step's own steps, and one line for the sessions (with Open session).
  • The dock: everything that was a card becomes a pill that opens a drawer over the stage: Documents, Branch & merge, Sessions, Build, Timeline, Usage. Nothing is lost; it is one click away instead of a scroll.
  • The rail folds to an icon strip on the Overview (tick Full rail to compare with today's); the other pages keep it as it is.
  • Fixes on the way: an idle session is waiting on you, not running; the ship orbits clear of its label; a title is said once.

What the app would need (not this board's job)

  • features.js overview() draws the stage (the journey card absolutely filling .fmain, without its max-width) and moves each card into a drawer; the other pages are untouched.
  • The walks keep their selectors (.overview, #journey, .action-main, .usage, the card headings inside the drawers).
  • The tray app's Tatty sits bottom right on the desktop, so the dock stays centred and the gate leaves that corner clear.
