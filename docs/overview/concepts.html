The idea. The Overview stops being a board of buttons and becomes a journey: where you are is the hero, the route is the centrepiece, and the one thing to do next is a single big button. Documents, timeline, usage, the branch and the housekeeping (Stages, Cancel, Archive, Refresh) go into a dock and a ⋯ menu.

The gate: manual Continue, Skip and Stop

When a stage closes, the workflow stops at a gate and nothing starts until you say so: Continue starts the next stage, Skip leaves it out and moves the gate to the stage after, Stop parks the feature (the session can end; Continue later picks it up). Skip is only offered for stages the workflow can leave out: Explore, Scope, Approach, Whole plan and Review. Requirements, Technical design, Sign-off, Breakdown and Build are required, so their Skip is greyed out with the reason.

What the app needs for this (toolbox and Tatty) is in continue-and-skip.md, beside this file.

The states
  • Running: a session is working on Scope. The ambient motion shows it is alive.
  • At a gate: Scope is closed, Approach is next and waiting for you.
  • Decisions: the session is waiting on three decisions.
  • Skipped: Scope was skipped at its gate; Technical design is running.
  • Left out: Explore and Whole plan were left out before starting (the stage picker); Build is at unit 4 of 6.
  • Complete: everything done and merged.

Tick Auto-finish stages to have running stages close on their own, and Auto-continue to also press Continue for you, which plays the whole journey.
