Feature Overview: themes

Round three. M1 Hairline is the free default, Star Chart and Expedition Map stay, and four new premium themes take the place of Steam Railway and Deep Dive. Everything runs the same fake feature through the same states.

What changed since round two. M1 Hairline is locked in as the free default; Segments and the Focus carousel are retired. Star Chart and Expedition Map stay for their simplicity: one distinct idea each, drawn with a few thin lines. Steam Railway and Deep Dive are gone, as too bright and too storybook. The four new premium themes, E to H, aim for sleek and thematic: a restrained palette (two or three colours and one light), one strong idea, hairline drawing, and motion that is slow rather than busy. Confetti is kept to a few flecks, or none at all.

How this page is laid out
  1. The free default (M1). Locked in. It uses the page's own colours and is the fallback for every other theme.
  2. Kept (A and B). Unchanged from round two.
  3. New premium themes (E to H). Shortlist the ones worth building; notes are saved in this browser.
  4. The shop. A large preview and a shelf of seven themes. Hover one to try it on; click to buy or wear it. You start with 1,250 coins.
