Feature Overview: themes

Round two. Three free defaults to pick one from, four themes to sell in the shop, and a shop mock to try them on. Everything runs the same fake feature through the same states.

Colours

What changed since round one. You liked the thematic directions (Star Chart, Expedition Map and a train, now a real steam railway), so they become things you buy from the shop. Everyone starts with one sleek, minimal default that is free. There is also a new idea, Deep Dive. Blueprint, Metro and Neon are gone.

How this page is laid out
  1. Free defaults (M1 to M3). They use the page's own colours, so they look native to Tatty in light and dark. Pick one with Use as the default; it becomes the shop's "Minimal" theme.
  2. Shop themes (A to D). The four paid ones, each with its own scenery, vocabulary on the buttons, and motion.
  3. The shop. A large preview, and a shelf of five themes. Hover one to try it on, click to buy or wear it. You start with 1,250 coins.

The gate (Continue, Skip, Stop) and the states are as in round one. What the app needs for themes is written up in themes-in-the-shop.md, beside this file.
